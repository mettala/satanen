Hakemisto 199: proposal (RP.11)

Teletext page 199 Päähakemistojen kooste as one scrolling list, read the way the teletext page is meant to be read: each yellow heading (UUTISET, URHEILU, ALUEUUTISET …) starts a group, and inside a group the left column is read first, then the right one. Every entry is its own row with its page number on the right, so Yle TV1 301 and Äänitekstitys 337 no longer interleave. The title and the 1/4 … 4/4 counters are shown. The Aakkosellinen hakemisto 196-198 line that Yle repeats at the bottom of every sub-page is shown once, as a card at the top. Group headings are links too (UUTISET opens 101). A row with one number is one link; a row with several (Yle Radio1 340, 345-346) has one underlined link per number, and a range opens its first page (as proposed in RP.7). Colour dots follow the drawer: Uutiset blue, Urheilu green, Sää ja liikenne purple, the rest grey. The previous/next card (RP.10) stays at the end of the page as on every page and is not drawn here.

Phones in the first two rows show one long page cut into consecutive screens (scroll inside any phone to move). Region names there use option C, the recommended one; the last two rows compare the three options.

Vaalea: the whole page, screen by screen

Tumma: the whole page, screen by screen

Region names: three options (owner decides)
