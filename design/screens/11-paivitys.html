Päivitä lista floats over the top of every feed list (and Etusivu) only when the new list differs. Pressing it scrolls to the top and swaps the list. Sivu päivitetty appears over an article when its text was replaced with a changed copy; it can't be tapped. In the article mock-ups the message loops: the bar runs down over 5 s, then the message is gone for a moment and returns, only so you can watch it.
